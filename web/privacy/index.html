The short version

  • There are no accounts. The game never asks for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your progress is saved on your phone. If Google backup is on, Android also keeps a copy in your own Google account backup so it comes back when you reinstall. We cannot see it.
  • Google services in the game handle a small amount of data so we can show ads, count how levels are played, fix crashes, send notifications and process purchases.
  • You can turn off analytics, crash reports and notifications in the game's Settings. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you choose whether ads may be personalised.

1. Who we are

Stone Storm is made and published by Intercore, a sole proprietorship based in India ("we", "us"). We decide how the data described here is used and are responsible for it. You can reach us at intercoreapp@gmail.com.

3. Advertising and consent

The game shows a short full-screen ad between some levels, and video ads that you choose to watch for a reward. Ads never appear in the middle of a level or in your first session. Buying Remove Ads stops all of them.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before any ad loads, Google's consent form asks whether ads may use your data for personalisation. You can change your answer at any time from Settings → Ad Privacy in the game.
  • US states with privacy laws: where Google shows a privacy message for your state, Settings → Ad Privacy also lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of your data for targeted ads.
  • Everywhere: you can delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your phone). After that, you only see ads based on the game and general location.

All ads are limited to Google's PG rating (suitable for general audiences with parental guidance). Read how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Analytics and crash reports

The game only sends events from a fixed list. It drops any other event before it leaves your phone. Events never contain your name, email, contacts, payment details or anything you type.

  • Game start and end, session count and length, days since install
  • Level started, completed, failed, retried or left from the pause menu, with level number, attempt, stars, score, time, shots and heroes used
  • Tutorial started and finished
  • Rewarded ad watched or failed to load, and full-screen ad shown, with the level and reward
  • Purchases started, completed or failed (product and reward only), crates opened, coins spent on upgrades and skins
  • Daily spin used and notification opened
  • Which feature card was shown on the home screen and whether it was tapped
  • Google Play rating card requested and update offered (with the new version number)

Firebase also records a few standard events on its own, such as first open and app update. Crash reports are sent the next time you open the game after a crash.

You can turn off both analytics and crash reports in Settings → Analytics. Nothing more is collected until you turn it back on.

5. Notifications

On Android 13 and later, the game asks for permission before showing any notification. We send two kinds:

  • Reminders, such as your free daily spin being ready. Your phone schedules these itself, and no data leaves the device to create them.
  • News, such as a new event or levels. These are sent through Firebase Cloud Messaging to everyone subscribed to a topic. We never target a single player.

Turning off Settings → Reminders cancels scheduled reminders and unsubscribes your phone from every news topic. You can also block notifications for the game in Android settings.

6. Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play. Your card or other payment details go to Google and never reach us. The game receives a purchase record (product, order ID and purchase token) to give you the item and to restore Remove Ads. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.

Ratings and updates

The game can ask Google Play to show its rating card after you win a level, and can ask Google Play whether a newer version of the game exists. Both run inside the Google Play Store app on your phone. Your rating and review go to Google Play under your Play account, and we only see what Google shows on the public store page.

7. Data kept on your device

Your levels, stars, coins, upgrades, skins and settings are stored in a save file inside the game's private storage on your phone. We cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its storage in Android settings. If backup is turned on in your phone settings, Android saves a copy of this one file in your Google account backup (encrypted by Google, not sent to us) and puts it back when you reinstall the game or move to a new phone. You can turn this off in Android Settings → Google → Backup.

9. Sharing and selling

We only share data with the Google services listed in section 2, which process it to provide those services. We do not sell your personal data for money. Under some US state laws, letting an ad network use your advertising ID for personalised ads counts as "selling" or "sharing". You can opt out as described in section 3. We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

10. Your choices

  • Analytics and crash reports: Settings → Analytics in the game.
  • Reminders and news: Settings → Reminders in the game, or Android notification settings.
  • Ad consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland, some US states): Settings → Ad Privacy in the game.
  • Personalised ads anywhere: delete or reset your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • All ads: buy Remove Ads.
  • Everything on your phone: uninstall the game or clear its storage.

11. Retention and deletion

  • Analytics data linked to an installation is kept by Google for up to 14 months, then deleted. Summary reports with no identifiers may be kept longer.
  • Crash reports are kept for 90 days.
  • Push tokens that are not used for 270 days are removed by Firebase.
  • Ad data is kept by Google as described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

To ask us to delete analytics and crash data linked to your installation, email intercoreapp@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". Include the game version (shown at the bottom of Settings) and roughly when you played. We will ask Google to delete the data for that installation within 30 days and confirm by email. Uninstalling the game also stops all collection and creates new random identifiers if you install it again.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • see the personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • correct it, or have it deleted;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing;
  • object to or restrict some processing;
  • opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data and of targeted advertising;
  • appoint someone to act for you;
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

These rights come from laws such as the EU and UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, and US state privacy laws including California's CCPA. Email us to use any of them. We reply within 30 days and will not treat you differently for using your rights. Because the game has no accounts, we may ask for your installation details so we can find your data.

Grievances (India): Intercore is the contact for grievances under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023. Write to intercoreapp@gmail.com. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

13. Legal bases and international transfers

For people in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, we rely on:

  • consent for personalised ads and for notifications;
  • legitimate interests for analytics, crash reports, non-personalised ads and fraud prevention, which keep the game working and free to play;
  • performance of a contract for purchases.

Google may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Google uses safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses for these transfers.

14. Security

All data the game sends travels encrypted over HTTPS. Firebase and AdMob data is stored on Google's infrastructure and protected by Google's security measures. We only see reports in the Firebase and AdMob consoles, which are protected by the developer account sign-in. No system is perfectly secure, but we collect as little as we can to keep the risk low.

15. Children

Stone Storm has a cartoon style and is rated for a general audience, but it is not directed at children under 13. In Google Play it is listed for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game and data about them was collected, email us and we will delete it.

16. This website

This site has no cookies, analytics, ads or forms. It is hosted by Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare processes your IP address and browser details to deliver the pages and protect them from attacks, under the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

17. Changes to this policy

If the game starts handling data differently, we will update this page and the effective date at the top before the change reaches the game.
