Five heroes, five ways to break a wall

Each hero rides the catapult and has one trick. Tap mid-flight to use it, and pick the right one for the fortress in front of you.

  • Rocky

    Slams straight down with two and a half times his weight. Good against stone and archers.

  • Blaze

    Dashes forward and bursts into flames. Fire spreads along every wooden beam it touches.

  • Boomer

    Explodes on a tap, or by himself a moment after landing. Clears everything nearby.

  • Twister

    Turns into a whirlwind that lifts light blocks and guards, then flings them away.

  • Quaky

    Hits the ground and sends a shockwave through the supports of a tower.
