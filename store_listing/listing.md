# Play Store listing: copy and paste

Paste these into **Play Console → Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing**.
Images are in this folder. Rules: no "best", "#1", "free gems" or prices in the text, no
other game names, and no wording that suggests rewards the game doesn't give.

## App name (max 30 characters)
```
Stone Storm
```

## Short description (max 80 characters)
```
Fling heroes from your catapult and smash cartoon castles to pieces!
```

## Full description (max 4000 characters)
```
Load the catapult, aim, and send your heroes flying into enemy fortresses. Knock down towers, set off powder barrels and bring the whole castle crashing down before the enemy archers wear down your army.

FIVE HEROES, FIVE WAYS TO BREAK A WALL
• Rocky slams straight through stone.
• Blaze dashes and sets wood on fire.

• Twister lifts loose pieces and throws them.

Tap during the flight to use each hero's trick, and pick the right one for every fortress.

75 LEVELS IN EIGHT REALMS
Cherry blossom valleys, desert canyons, pirate harbours, crystal mines, floating sky islands, the Scarlet Highlands, the Storm Crags and the Iron Citadel. Every realm ends with a boss fortress, and new realms are on the way.

REAL PHYSICS, REAL CHAIN REACTIONS
Seesaws, rolling logs, runaway wheels, wrecking balls, rope bridges, flood gates, magnets, portals, gravity fields, bumpers and repair crystals. Hit the right spot and watch one shot bring down everything.

MORE TO DO

Stone Storm is free to play. It contains ads and optional in-app purchases. Everything in the levels can be beaten without paying.
```
