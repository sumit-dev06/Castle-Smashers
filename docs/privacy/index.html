Castle Smashers – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026
Developer: Sumit (individual developer)
Contact: CONTACT_EMAIL

Castle Smashers ("the game") is a single-player game for Android. We do not ask for your name, email or any account. Your game progress is stored only on your device. This policy explains the data that the services built into the game process.

1. Data the game collects

DataWho processes itWhy
Advertising ID, device and app information, approximate location from IP address, ad interactions Google AdMobTo show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first (Google User Messaging Platform) and can choose non-personalised ads.
App usage events (levels started, completed or failed, ads watched, purchases made, session length), device model, Android version, app version Google Firebase AnalyticsTo understand which levels are too hard and improve the game. Events never include your name, contacts or payment details.
Crash logs (stack trace, device model, Android version, app version, recent game events) Google Firebase CrashlyticsTo find and fix crashes.
Purchase records (product, order and purchase token) Google Play BillingTo process in-app purchases and restore Remove Ads. Payment details are handled by Google Play and never reach us.

2. Notifications

The game can show reminders (daily spin, daily reward, new content). They are created on your device; no notification data is sent to us. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in Android settings.

3. Your choices

  • Turn analytics and crash reporting off in Settings → Analytics.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Google → Ads).
  • Change ad consent (EEA/UK) from the consent form shown at start.
  • Remove all game data by uninstalling the game or clearing its storage.

4. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Ads are limited to teen-appropriate content.

5. Retention and deletion

Analytics data is kept by Google for up to 14 months. Crash reports are kept for 90 days. To ask for deletion of analytics data linked to your device, email us with the app version and approximate date you played.
