# About Castle Smashers

## One line
Fling characterful projectiles from a catapult to topple cartoon fortresses and defeat the enemy
archers before they shoot down your soldiers.

## Story
The **Iron Baron** has seized the five realms and filled them with fortresses manned by his
**Redcap Archers**. The **Vanguard**, a small band of brave, round-bellied soldiers, marches to
take the land back. They have one catapult and four unlikely heroes to load into it:

| Hero | Projectile | Personality |
|---|---|---|
| **Rocky** | Stone | Grumpy, dependable, hits like a truck |
| **Blaze** | Fire pot | Hot-headed and impatient, sets wood ablaze |
| **Boomer** | Bomb | Cheerful, short fuse, loves loud noises |
| **Twister** | Cyclone | Dizzy, giggly, a little whirlwind that lifts everything |

Each realm ends at a bigger fortress. The last one is the Baron's Iron Citadel.

## Audience
- Casual mobile players aged 13+ (the store listing targets 13+ so we stay out of the Google Play
  Families programme and its stricter ad rules; the content itself is cartoon and family friendly).
- Short sessions: one level takes 30–90 seconds.

## Design pillars
1. **Satisfying destruction.** Every hit should look, sound and feel heavy: shake, dust, splinters,
   creaks, slow-motion collapses.
2. **Readable at a glance.** Bright colours, thick outlines, clear materials (wood = brown planks,
   stone = grey bricks, ice/glass none in v1), enemies always visible.
3. **A little pressure.** Archers shoot back between your shots. Taking them out first matters.
4. **Characters with personality.** Projectiles, soldiers and archers blink, react and taunt.
5. **Fair monetisation.** The whole campaign can be finished without paying. Ads are opt-in
   (rewarded) except a capped interstitial between levels, which "Remove Ads" removes.

## Platform
Android phones and tablets, landscape, Android 7.0+ (API 24) minimum, OpenGL ES 3.0.
Distributed on Google Play only.
